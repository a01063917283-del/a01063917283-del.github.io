Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy describes how Landmark Decoded Uploader (“the app”) handles information. The app is used only by the owner of the Landmark Decoded YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information the app accesses

  • Basic YouTube channel information (channel name and ID) to confirm the correct channel is connected.
  • Permission to upload videos and set video metadata (title, description, tags, thumbnail, privacy status) on the connected channel.

How information is used and stored

The app uses this access only to upload the channel owner's own videos. The OAuth access token is stored only on the owner's private server and is never shared, sold or transferred to third parties. The app does not collect data from viewers or from any other YouTube users, and does not use cookies or analytics.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. After revocation, stored tokens stop working and are deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Landmark Decoded channel page.
